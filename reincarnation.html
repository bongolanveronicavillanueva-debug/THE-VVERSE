Again Met You

Author: VV

Synopsis: A young woman remembers her past life's tragic promise upon brushing past a familiar stranger amidst a shower of cherry blossoms.

Chapter 1: The Sakura Vow

Pink cherry blossom petals swirled gently in the spring breeze, blanketing the cobblestone path leading up to the ancient shrine. I walked slowly, pulling my coat tighter against the chilly morning air. For as long as I could remember, I had been having the exact same recurring nightmare every single night of a burning castle and a tragic vow.

As I reached the top of the shrine steps, a tall guy walking down the path accidentally brushed past my shoulder. The moment our skin touched, an electric shock shot straight through my entire body.

My books dropped to the ground, scattering across the stone floor. The stranger froze and quickly crouched down to help me pick them up. As he handed me my notebook, our eyes locked.

"I'm so sorry about that," he said, offering a warm, gentle smile. "My name is Julian. Have we... met somewhere before?"

Tears started streaming down my face. "Yes," I choked out. "We promised we would find each other again under the cherry blossoms."

Julian’s smile slowly faded into a cold smirk. He leaned in closer and whispered, "I know, Lyra. I've been hunting down your past-life incarnations for centuries just to make sure none of them remember the truth—you were the one who betrayed our kingdom."
