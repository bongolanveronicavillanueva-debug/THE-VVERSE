Still, It's You

Author: VV

Synopsis: Two estranged lovers meet atop a hill during sunset after five years of separation, realizing that time never really erased their unsaid promises.

Chapter 1: The Sunset Hill

The wind was chilly as I stood on top of Sunset Hill. I looked down at my watch—it was almost five in the afternoon. The sky was slowly changing into shades of orange, pink, and purple. It was beautiful, but my heart was beating so fast I could barely breathe.

It had been five long years. Five years since Clara walked out of my life to pursue her dreams in Europe. We didn't break up with screaming or crying. We just sat on this exact same wooden bench, looked at each other, and accepted that our paths were going in different directions. She wanted the big city life, and I had to stay behind to manage our family business.

Every single day after she left, I tried to convince myself that I was over her. I dated other people, focused on work, and deleted her photos. But every time sunset came, my mind always went back to her laugh, her voice, and the way her hand felt in mine.

"You're still as punctual as ever, Mateo," a quiet voice said from behind me.

My breath caught in my throat. I didn't even need to turn around to know who it was. That soft, gentle tone was something I could recognize anywhere in the world. Slowly, I turned around.

There she was. Clara was wearing a cream-colored coat, her long dark hair swaying gently in the breeze. She looked more mature, more confident, but her eyes were still the exact same eyes I fell in love with back in college.

"Hey," I managed to say, offering a nervous smile. "You actually came."

"Of course I did," she replied, stepping closer until she was standing just a few feet away from me. "When you sent that message asking to meet here, I didn't even hesitate. I booked a flight right away."

We stood there for a moment in silence, just staring at each other while the wind blew around us. There was so much history between us, so many unspoken words and wasted years.

"Why now, Clara?" I asked, my voice cracking slightly. "Why did you agree to see me after five years of total silence?"

Clara lowered her head for a second, then reached into her pocket. She pulled out a small, worn-out paper star—the same kind of star she used to fold for me whenever I was stressed out during exams.

"Because I realized that no matter how far I ran, I was never really happy," she whispered, looking up at me with tears pooling in her eyes. "I got the fame, the successful career, and the life I thought I wanted. But every night when I went to sleep, I felt completely empty. Because the person I wanted to share everything with was still standing on this hill."

Hearing those words felt like a heavy weight lifting off my chest. I took two steps forward, closing the distance between us, and gently took her cold hands in mine.

"I never stopped waiting for you, Clara," I admitted softly. "Not even for a single day."

A tear rolled down her cheek, and she let out a quiet laugh before stepping right into my chest, wrapping her arms tightly around my waist. I held her close, feeling her warm heartbeat against mine. The sun finally vanished below the horizon, but for the first time in five years, my world didn't feel dark anymore.
