The Girl Who Sees Ghosts

Author: VV

Synopsis: A young medium wanders into a misty forest, helping lost spirits pass into the afterlife, until she uncovers a terrifying secret about her own reflection.

Chapter 1: Azure Spirits

The Whispering Woods had always been strictly off-limits to ordinary villagers after sundown. But for me, twilight was the only hour when the chaotic world truly made sense. As a born medium, my eyes perceived what everyone else ignored: luminescent blue wisps dancing between ancient oak trunks like living stardust.

My name is Ren. For years, I spent my quiet nights walking through these dark woods, helping lost souls find peace so they could finally pass on to the afterlife. I never feared them; to me, they were just tired souls looking for a way home.

Tonight, the mist rolling through the valley was much thicker than usual. A soft, glowing blue spirit floated gracefully in front of me, shaping itself into the form of a young woman sitting quietly near the edge of a mossy riverbank.

"Are you lost?" I asked gently, stepping closer and holding out my hand. "Don't be afraid. I can help you find your way home."

The spirit slowly raised her head, turning to look directly at me. The bright blue spectral light illuminated her face with chilling clarity.

I stumbled backward, dropping my wooden staff into the mud as my breath caught in my throat. The spirit sitting on the riverbank was wearing my exact jacket, my exact boots, and possessed my exact face. Down at the bottom of the river, floating motionless beneath the crystal-clear water, was my own lifeless body, clutching a silver pocket watch that had permanently stopped at 7:00 PM.

The spirit smiled sadly at me and whispered softly into the freezing wind: "You've been helping everyone else cross over, Ren... but you forgot that you drowned in this river three days ago."
