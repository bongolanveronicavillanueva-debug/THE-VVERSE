Chapter 1: The Final Envelope

The distant thunder of artillery echoed across the golden wheat fields of Oakhaven, disturbing the peaceful quiet of the countryside. Sitting on an ammunition crate beside his dugout trench, Arthur smoothed out a crumpled piece of parchment using his dirt-stained fingers.

He dipped his fountain pen into a small bottle of ink, watching his hand tremble slightly as wind blew dry leaves and loose envelopes across the soil.

*"Dearest Eleanor,"* Arthur wrote, his words carefully penned under the pale afternoon sun. *"If this letter reaches your hands before I do, know that every step I took across these ruined fields was guided by the hope of returning to your side."*

A loud bugle call resonated from the command tent, signaling the regiment to assemble. Arthur carefully folded the parchment, sealed it inside a brown envelope, and handed it to the young messenger boy running past the trenches.

"Deliver it to the valley post, kid," Arthur said, adjusting his steel helmet as he stood up to face the horizon.
