The Mind's Lie

Author: VV

Synopsis: A psychiatrist reviewing his own clinical records late at night discovers that his entire life and memories might be completely fabricated.

Chapter 1: Fractured Perception

Dr. Alan Vance stared into the vanity mirror of his dimly lit office, looking closely at a reflection that felt subtly wrong. The ticking wall clock sounded distorted, stretching each second into an agonizing eternity.

My name is Alan. I spent the last ten years helping patients cure their delusions and memory gaps. But tonight, sitting alone in my office at 2:00 AM, I started questioning my own sanity.

I pulled out my confidential patient ledger and opened it to Patient #409—a man who claimed his entire life was a controlled experiment scripted by someone else. But as I scanned the handwriting on the page, my blood ran instantly cold.

The notes were written in my own handwriting, detailing my exact daily schedule, my thoughts, and my fears down to the very second.

"You're finally noticing the fracture in your reality, aren't you, Alan?" a calm, familiar voice echoed from the empty leather armchair behind my desk.

I turned around slowly, my heart pounding. Sitting in the chair was a man who looked exactly like me, smiling calmly. "Who are you?" I gasped, my vision blurring as the room started spinning around me.
