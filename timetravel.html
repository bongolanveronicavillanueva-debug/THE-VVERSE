The Time We Met

Author: VV

Synopsis: A scientist uses a brass pocket watch device to travel back ten years in time to save his wife from a tragic accident, uncovering an impossible timeline loop.

Chapter 1: The Chrono Key

The gigantic brass gears of the Greenwich Observatory clock tower hummed with intense temporal energy, casting rotating shadows across the wooden floor. I held my brass pocket watch-chronometer tightly in my hand, setting the gear dial to *'2016-10-14'*.

My name is Ethan. For ten long years, I lived in absolute grief after my wife, Maya, died in a sudden car crash at the central highway intersection. I built this time travel key in secret, sacrificing my career and health just for a single chance to undo that tragic night.

"If you turn that key, Ethan, the temporal loop will close," my research assistant warned over the communicator headset. "You only have one shot."

Without hesitating, I turned the brass gear. A vortex of golden light swallowed the room, spinning around me until the air smelled like rain and ozone. When the light faded, I was standing on the wet pavement of the central intersection on October 14, 2016 at 8:55 PM—five minutes before the crash.

I spotted Maya’s red car stopped at the red light across the street. Heart racing with joy, I ran out onto the road, waving my arms frantically. "Maya! Stop! Don't drive!" I screamed.

Maya looked out her window, startled by a stranger shouting in the rain. Confused and distracted by me, she didn't see the heavy truck speeding down the slippery hill toward the intersection.

Tires screeched loudly as the truck crashed into her car right in front of my eyes. I froze in utter terror as the horrifying realization broke my mind—she didn't die because of a random accident ten years ago. She died because I traveled back in time and distracted her.
