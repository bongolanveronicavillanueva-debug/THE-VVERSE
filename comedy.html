Accidentally Famous

Author: VV

Synopsis: A clumsy street photographer takes a silly picture of a stylish cat in a park, accidentally starting the biggest internet craze of the year.

Chapter 1: The Viral Paws

If there was an award for being the clumsiest person alive, I would win it without a doubt. Just this morning, I managed to trip over my own shoelaces, drop my breakfast sandwich face-down on the pavement, and spill hot coffee all over my favorite white jacket.

My name is Toby, and I'm a freelance street photographer struggling to pay rent in downtown Manhattan. To clear my mind after my disastrous morning, I sat down on a quiet park bench and pulled out my vintage camera to clean the lens.

That was when it happened. A fat, grumpy-looking orange tabby cat walked over to my bench, hopped up right next to me, and casually rested its paw on my knee. To make things ten times better, someone had put a pair of tiny gold aviator sunglasses on its face.

The cat yawned dramatically, stretching its front paws out like a cool rockstar posing for an album cover.

"Well, aren't you a stylish little guy," I laughed, lifting my camera and snapping a quick photo just for fun.

Without thinking much about it, I uploaded the picture to my social media profile with the caption: *'Found my new life coach in the park today.'* Then I went home and took a well-deserved nap.

Three hours later, my phone started vibrating on my nightstand. It didn't just vibrate—it buzzed so violently it started sliding across the wooden table like a runaway bug.

I groggily picked it up and opened the app. My eyes went wide. The photo had over five million likes, thousands of comments, and my follower count was shooting up faster than a rocket ship.

My phone rang loudly. It was my manager screaming into the receiver: "Toby! What did you do?! Jimmy Fallon's talk show producers are trying to find you, and Billboard Magazine wants an interview with the owner of the coolest cat on the internet!"

I stared at my phone screen in absolute shock. The best part? I didn't even own the cat—I had no idea whose pet it was!
