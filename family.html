Forever Us

Author: VV

Synopsis: A father and his young daughter return to their abandoned ancestral farmhouse, discovering a message left behind in the floorboards.

Chapter 1: Golden Fields

The afternoon sun cast a warm golden hue over the rolling hills of Willow Creek Farm. My name is Daniel. After spending ten stressful years working in the city, I decided to bring my seven-year-old daughter, Lily, back to restore my late parents' abandoned farmhouse.

We walked through the tall wild grass toward the front porch. Lily held my hand tightly, looking around with bright, curious eyes. "Daddy, is this where you used to play when you were a little boy?" she asked.

"Yes, sweetheart," I smiled, pushing open the squeaking front door. The house smelled faintly of old wood and dried lavender.

While Lily explored the living room, I started sweeping the dusty floorboards near the fireplace. My broom hit a loose wooden plank. Curious, I knelt down and pried the plank open with my pocketknife.

Inside the cavity was a small metal tin box. I opened it and pulled out a letter written in my mother's handwriting, dated last week.

I unfolded the paper and read: "Daniel, if you are reading this, it means you finally came back home just like I prayed. I am sorry I couldn't wait for you, but remember that family is built on love, not bricks."

I stared at the letter in shock, my hands trembling. My mother had passed away three years ago—long before this letter was supposedly written.
