Blackwell House

Author: VV

Synopsis: A young woman explores the dark mansion she inherited from her grandfather, only to realize the shadows inside the house are watching her every move.

Chapter 1: The Whispering Mirrors

The fog outside was so thick I could barely see the rusty iron gates behind me. I stood on the porch of Blackwell House, holding an old brass lantern that flickered weakly against the darkness. The old wood smelled like rot and wet soil.

My name is Evelyn. Last week, I received a letter saying my grandfather had passed away and left this entire estate to me. I had never met him, and the people in town stared at me with terrified eyes when I asked for directions. *"Don't go there after sunset, girl,"* an old man at the gas station warned me. *"That house eats anyone who stays past dark."*

I thought it was just silly town superstition. But as soon as I pushed the front door open, a freezing cold gust of wind blew past me, making the old floorboards groan like someone was crying.

I stepped inside the grand foyer. The air was heavy and suffocating. Dust motes danced in the yellow glow of my lantern. On the walls, old black-and-white family portraits hung in dirty golden frames. I walked past them slowly, but every time I moved, I felt like the eyes in the paintings were following me.

I stopped in front of a giant antique mirror standing in the middle of the hallway. The glass was old and scratched. I raised my lantern to inspect my reflection. I looked pale, exhausted, and terrified.

I took a deep breath and turned around to leave. But then I froze.

In the mirror reflection behind me... my reflection didn't turn around.

My heart stopped beating. Cold sweat broke out across my forehead. Slowly, I looked back at the mirror over my shoulder. My reflection in the glass was still staring directly at me, a creepy, unnatural smile spreading across her face.

Then, the reflection raised her hand and pressed her palm flat against the inside of the glass. A dark, bloody handprint slowly smeared across the surface.

"Welcome home, Evelyn..." a raspy, distorted whisper echoed right into my ear, even though nobody was standing near me.

Before I could even scream, the flame inside my brass lantern blew out completely, leaving me trapped in pitch-black darkness with whatever was inside that mirror.
