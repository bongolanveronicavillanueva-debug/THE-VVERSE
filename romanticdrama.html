Author: VV

Synopsis: An intense gaze shared on a misty evening street ignites a passion caught between rival family empires.

Chapter 1: Misty Intersection

Streetlights flickered through the evening mist as Julian stepped out of his black sedan. Standing under the awning of the coffee shop across the street was Serena—the daughter of his family's fiercest business rival.

She looked up, their eyes locking across the quiet, damp street. Despite the warnings from their families, Julian crossed the street, stopping inches away from her.

"You shouldn't be here, Julian," Serena whispered softly. "I'm done letting our past dictate our future," Julian replied, gently holding her hand.
