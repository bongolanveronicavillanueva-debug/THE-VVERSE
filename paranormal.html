Chapter 1: The Blood Moon Call

A piercing, majestic howl echoed across the silver-lit expanse of Frostwood Ridge, sending instant shivers down my spine. Thick snow fell softly around my winter boots as I trekked deeper into the frozen wilderness, searching for the ancient ruins described in my grandfather's folklore scrolls.

My name is Aaron. I always thought werewolf legends were just bedtime stories told to scare kids. But tonight, as the full moon shone like a giant silver coin in the dark sky, I was about to find out how wrong I was.

Suddenly, the snow underfoot trembled. From behind a snow-draped boulder, a massive silver wolf stepped into the clearing. Its bright amber eyes glowed with fierce intelligence and an undeniable supernatural authority.

I froze completely, holding my breath as the giant beast padded silently toward me. But instead of attacking, the wolf bowed its head. In a flash of warm light, the creature transformed into a tall, striking man with silver hair and glowing amber eyes, wearing dark leather garments.

"The Silver-Fang pack has waited three centuries for your soul to awaken, Aaron," the Alpha spoke smoothly, stepping closer as a strange, unexplainable warmth connected our hearts.
