Chapter 1: The Golden Hour

The 4:00 PM school bell rang across Oakridge High. While everyone else rushed home, I stayed behind in Room 204 to clean the chalkboards. Under desk #12, I found a rusted key and an old note dated ten years ago: *"Please help me, I am locked inside the basement storage room."*

I used the key to open the classroom's old wooden trapdoor, shining my flashlight down into the dark staircase. Resting on the bottom step was my own blue backpack.

Suddenly, the classroom door slammed shut, and a cold hand pushed me down the dark stairs as a whisper hissed: "Thanks for bringing back the key to my cell."
