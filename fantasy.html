The Last Heir

Author: VV

Synopsis: A exiled young prince returns to his ruined castle under a full moon, ready to take back his kingdom from the dark forces that stole his family's crown.

Chapter 1: The Moonlit Spire

The wind was freezing as I stood at the edge of the cliff, staring up at Castle Valenore. Ten years ago, this place was full of light, laughter, and music. But tonight, it looked like a dark monster sleeping under the moonlight, surrounded by mist and dead trees.

I pulled my black cloak tighter around my shoulders. My name is Kaelen, and I am the last living prince of Eldoria. Ten years ago, a dark warlord named Malakor attacked our kingdom in the middle of the night. He killed my father, destroyed our armies, and took the throne for himself. I was only twelve years old when my guards rushed me out of the castle through a hidden tunnel.

I spent ten years surviving in the shadows, training every day until my hands were full of calluses and my body was covered in battle scars. I wasn't that scared little boy anymore. Tonight, I came back to finish what was started.

"Kaelen," a quiet voice spoke from the darkness behind me.

I didn't panic. I slowly turned around to see Master Elowen, the old wizard who had been secretly protecting me all these years. He held a wooden staff that glowed with a faint blue light.

"The blood moon is almost full, my prince," Elowen warned me, his face looking worried. "Inside those gates, Malakor commands hundreds of shadow soldiers. Once you cross those doors, there is no turning back."

"I know, Elowen," I replied, grabbing the handle of my sword. "I didn't survive ten years in hiding just to run away now. Our people are suffering. I have to do this."

Elowen smiled proudly and nodded. He raised his staff and touched it against my chest. A warm wave of magical energy rushed through my blood, waking up the dragon-fire power that had been asleep inside me for years.

"Then go, Prince Kaelen. Claim your birthright and bring back the light to Eldoria," he whispered.

I walked up to the massive wooden doors of the castle. I placed my right palm against the cold iron surface. The royal runes carved into the wood began to glow bright blue, recognizing my royal bloodline. With a loud rumble, the heavy doors opened.

I drew my sword, its blade igniting with brilliant golden flames. In the center of the dark hall, sitting on my father's throne, Malakor opened his glowing red eyes and smirked.

"You took your time, little prince," Malakor sneered, drawing his dark blade.

"I'm here for my kingdom," I shouted, stepping into the hall as my golden fire illuminated the dark room. "And I'm taking it back tonight!"
