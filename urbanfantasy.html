Chapter 1: Midnight Runes

Neon rain flickered across the skyscraper rooftops of New Haven as I balanced on the edge of a ledge fifty stories above the busy street traffic. Beneath my fingerless leather gloves, glowing purple runes hummed with raw arcana magic.

My name is Zachary. To ordinary people commuting in the subway below, I’m just a guy who hangs around rooftops wearing a dark hoodie. But in the hidden realm, I am a Warden—a mage tasked with slaying shadow beasts that feed on human fear.

Tonight, a massive Shadow Drake was stalking the alleyways between the financial towers, preparing to strike a crowded night bus. I jumped off the ledge, casting an ethereal magic circle under my boots to soften my landing.

I unsheathed my arcana blade, its purple flames illuminating the dark alley. "Hey, ugly! Pick on someone who can fight back!" I shouted, slashing through the monster's tail.

The beast shrieked in pain, turning its red eyes toward me. But as the arcana flames burned away its dark outer shadow skin, I froze in shock. Beneath the monster's shadowy form was the face of my younger brother who had gone missing two weeks ago.

The Wardens hadn't been hunting wild monsters—they were turning missing humans into shadow beasts all along.
