Chapter 1: The Sun Temple

The humidity inside the jungle was so thick it felt like breathing water. I wiped sweat from my forehead with my sleeve, using my machete to hack through another thick curtain of dangling vines. My boots were completely covered in mud, and my arms were scratched from thorns, but I couldn't stop walking.

My name is Leo. Ever since I was a kid, my grandfather told me stories about an uncharted island in the Pacific that hid the legendary Sun Temple—a sacred ruin built by an ancient civilization, powered by a glowing crystal that could illuminate an entire city.

According to my grandfather's hand-drawn map, I was finally standing right on top of it. I pushed aside the final layer of large ferns, and my breath caught in my throat.

Rising out of the green canopy was a massive, weathered stone pyramid covered in intricate carvings of suns and jaguars. Golden beams of afternoon sunlight pierced straight through the thick tree branches above, hitting the crystal stone sitting right at the peak of the pyramid.

"We actually found it, Leo," my partner Maya whispered in awe as she stepped out from the trees behind me, lowering her backpack.

"Yeah, we did," I smiled, pulling out my climbing gear. "Now comes the real fun part—let's just hope the ancient traps installed 500 years ago still aren't working."
