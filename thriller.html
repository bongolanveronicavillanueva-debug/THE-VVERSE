Synopsis: A reporter sneaks into a high-security skyscraper at midnight to steal proof of a massive corporate crime, only to realize he was trapped from the start.

Chapter 1: Shattered Mirrors

The crunch of broken glass under my boots sounded way too loud in the dead silence of the 45th floor. Outside the huge floor-to-ceiling windows, the city neon lights flashed in blue, purple, and red, casting eerie shadows across the abandoned office.

My palms were sweating inside my black gloves. My name is Marcus, and I used to be a respected journalist. But six months ago, after I started asking questions about the city's largest technology company, Vanguard Inc., I was fired and my reputation was ruined. They wanted me to shut up. But tonight, I was going to ruin them instead.

I crouched behind a heavy glass desk in the CEO’s private office. In the center of the room was a small metallic box connected to a glowing server tower. That little drive contained all the proof I needed—names, bank transfers, and illegal wiretaps that could send the entire board of directors to prison.

I pulled out a small hacking device and plugged it into the server drive. Green lines of code started scrolling down my phone screen.

'20% downloaded... 75% downloaded... 100% COMPLETE.'

"Got it!" I pulled the flash drive out and shoved it into my jacket pocket. But as soon as I stood up, a loud mechanical lock echoed through the room. The metal emergency shutters came sliding down over all the doors and windows, sealing me inside the office.

Then, all the lights went out completely.

A single red laser dot appeared on my chest, coming from the shadows near the entrance. A smooth voice spoke from the darkness: "Thank you for gathering all the evidence in one place, Marcus. You just saved us the trouble of hunting down your underground source."

My heart sank into my stomach as I realized the hacker who gave me the access codes was working for Vanguard Inc. all along.
