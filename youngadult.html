Chapter 1: The New Horizon

Standing on the rooftop of her apartment building, Mia watched the sun set over the city, turning the skyline into a sea of amber and purple. Her packed suitcases sat by the door inside.

Tomorrow she was moving to a new city to pursue her art scholarship. She pulled out her sketchbook, drawing one final outline of the city she grew up in.

"Scared?" her friend Sam asked. Mia smiled, closing her sketchbook. "A little bit. But mostly... excited for the next chapter."
