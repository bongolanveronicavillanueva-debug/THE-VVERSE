Synopsis: A high school girl stands under drifting cherry blossom petals on the school roof, finally gathering the courage to confess her feelings.

Chapter 1: Rooftop Breeze

The gentle spring wind blew bright pink sakura petals past the chain-link fence of the high school rooftop. I clutched a pink-sealed letter tightly against my sailor-uniform chest, my heart beating so loudly in my ears that I thought it might burst.

My name is Aoi. For the entire school year, I had been the typical quiet girl sitting in the back row of the classroom, watching Ren from afar. He was the kind-hearted class representative who always helped everyone with their homework, but to me, he was the center of my entire universe.

The heavy metal door creaked open, breaking my thoughts. Ren stepped out onto the roof, looking surprised to see me standing near the ledge.

"Aoi?" Ren asked, walking over with a gentle, friendly smile on his face. "Did you leave something behind in the classroom? The bell rang twenty minutes ago."

I took a deep, trembling breath, gripping the letter until my knuckles turned white. There was no turning back now.

"No, Ren... I didn't leave anything in class," I said, looking straight into his surprised eyes. "I called you up here because... because there's something important I've wanted to tell you all semester!"

Ren blinked in surprise, the gentle breeze blowing his dark hair across his forehead as he waited for me to speak.
