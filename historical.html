Chapter 1: The Imperial Seal

The golden lanterns along the grand stone corridors of the Sun Palace flickered softly in the midnight breeze. I stood alone upon the eastern balcony, looking out over the sleeping rooftops of the ancient capital of Valeria. My silk gown rustled quietly against the polished marble floor.

My name is Lady Catherine. For years, my family has served as royal advisors to the crown. But tonight, the palace was draped in a heavy, dangerous tension. Two days ago, the old Emperor passed away. Before drawing his final breath, he entrusted me with a secret document hidden behind the royal throne room—a decree naming the true heir to the throne.

The problem? The ambitious Prince Regent Malachi had already seized control of the royal guards and declared himself king. If he found out I possessed this document, my life wouldn't last another hour.

"My Lady," a quiet, hurried voice whispered from the dark shadows of the terrace archway. Lord Henry, my closest childhood friend and captain of the loyalist guard, stepped forward with a worried look on his face.

"Henry, what is it?" I asked, tightly clutching the scroll hidden inside my cloak.

"Prince Malachi's guards are searching the West Wing right now," Henry reported, his breathing heavy. "They're tearing apart every room looking for the imperial seal. We need to smuggle you out of the capital before dawn."

I looked down at the scroll in my hands, feeling the weight of the entire kingdom resting on my shoulders. "I'm not running away, Henry," I said firmly, staring into the dark courtyard below. "Malachi thinks he has won, but this scroll is going to change everything."
