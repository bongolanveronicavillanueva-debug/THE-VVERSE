The Vanishing Note

Author: VV

Synopsis: A young detective investigates an old abandoned manor during a heavy rainstorm, searching for a missing professor and a secret code that was never solved.

Chapter 1: The Crimson Threshold

The heavy rain was pounding relentlessly against the broken windows of Blackwood Manor. I held my flashlight tight, pointing the light beam through the thick dust in the air. Every time I took a step on the old wooden floorboards, a loud creak echoed throughout the silent house.

My name is Detective Julian. I’ve been working on cold cases for three years, but this one was different. Ten years ago, Professor Samuel Vance—a genius who was studying secret codes—suddenly vanished from this exact mansion. The police back then found no forced entry, no signs of struggle, and no body. It was like he just dissolved into thin air.

At the end of the grand corridor stood a heavy iron door unlike any other in the estate. Etched into its rusted surface was a strange, luminescent red cipher that pulsed weakly in the dark, as if responding to human breath.

"I wouldn't touch that if I were you, Detective," a cold voice suddenly sounded from behind me.

I quickly spun around, holding my flashlight high. Standing near the top of the stairs was a person wearing a long black coat, their face hidden under a deep hood. "Where is Professor Vance?" I demanded.

"The Professor paid the ultimate price for curiosity," the figure replied softly. "Check your coat pocket, Julian." I pulled out a blank paper from my pocket, and glowing red words slowly appeared: *'The door itself is the trap.'*
