The Little Things

Author: VV

Synopsis: A lonely woman writes down small daily memories in her diary inside a rainy corner café, unravelling a subtle and eerie truth about her quiet routine.

Chapter 1: Morning Coffee

The heavy patter of rain created a cold, gentle rhythm against the large glass window of my favorite corner in the café. I sat in my familiar seat, slowly sipping my hot hazelnut coffee, feeling the steam fade as I opened my worn leather journal.

My name is Clara. In a world that constantly rushes, filled with noise and an endless race against time, this quiet hour every morning is my only sanctuary. Every day, exactly at 8:00 AM, I walk to table #4, order the exact same drink, and write down three small things I am grateful for that day.

For this morning, I wrote down:

1. The warm aroma of hazelnut coffee in my cup.
2. The relaxing sound of rain outside the window.
3. The kind and smiling waiter who always brings my order.

A gentle smile touched my lips as I closed my notebook. The familiar waiter walked over holding a slice of cinnamon cake. "On the house today, Miss Clara," he smiled warmly before walking back to the counter.

I reached out for my fork to taste the cake, but as I glanced at the café's reflection in the large window glass, the smile completely vanished from my lips. There was nobody at the counter. There were no running coffee machines, no active lights, and not a single customer around me.

With trembling hands, I looked down at the mug in my hand. It was ice-cold and filled entirely with thick dust. This old café had been boarded up and burned down three years ago on the night I never made it out alive.
