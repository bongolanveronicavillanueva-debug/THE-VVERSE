Chapter 1: The Final Shot

The rhythmic squeak of sneakers on polished hardwood echoed off the empty gymnasium walls as I spun the basketball between my hands. My name is Mark, wearing jersey #11. Six months ago, a severe knee injury threatened to end my basketball career forever.

Tomorrow was the national championship, and I spent every morning in grueling physical therapy just to run again. My teammate Kai walked over, handing me a medical tablet. "Mark... I checked your clinic files. Your knee never healed. The nerve blockers are the only reason you're standing."

I looked at my leg in horror as the numbness instantly wore off, collapsing onto the hardwood just as the stadium lights flickered out.
