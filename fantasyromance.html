Royal's Secret

Author: VV

Synopsis: A noblewoman guards a dangerous royal decree on a palace balcony, trying to stop a civil war.

Chapter 1: The Imperial Seal

The golden lanterns along the corridors of the Sun Palace flickered softly in the cool night breeze. My name is Lady Catherine. Standing upon the eastern balcony, I clutched a hidden scroll stamped with the late Emperor’s wax signature—a document that named the true heir to Valeria.

Lord Henry stepped out of the shadows, looking panicked. "My Lady, the guards are searching the palace! Prince Regent Malachi is coming!"

"We must protect the true heir," I said firmly, handing him the scroll. "Take this to the southern provinces immediately."

Henry bowed, took the scroll, and rushed down the stairs. But a moment later, Prince Malachi stepped onto the balcony, clapping slowly with a sinister grin.

"Thank you for flushing out the last loyalist, Catherine," Malachi laughed. "Henry was working for me the entire time. You just handed my trap right to the rebels."
